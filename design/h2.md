# H2: Thirty-minute convergence after the U.S. open

## Research question

H2 tests whether SKHY moves toward the H1 cross-market equilibrium after the
09:30 ET U.S. open. The canonical implementation is
`src/hypotheses/h2.py`. All estimation, liquidity qualification, convergence
events, and response calculations use completed 30-minute intervals.

The raw U.S. inputs arrive as shorter bars and quotes, but the model does not
estimate a shorter-horizon response. Six source bars are aggregated into each
30-minute OHLCV observation: first open, last close, summed volume and
transactions, and volume-weighted VWAP. Quotes and news are independently
bucketed over the same interval.

## Fixed and liquidity-qualified clocks

Model A begins at 09:30 ET. For interval `t`, the static equilibrium error is

\[
\boxed{
g^{static}_{d,t}
=\ln P^A_{d,t}
-\hat c_d
-\hat\theta_d
\ln\left(\frac{P^K_{d,last}}{10FX_{d,t}}\right)
}.
\]

`P^A` is the last valid SKHY bid/ask midpoint observed within the completed
30-minute interval. Missing quote intervals remain missing; no stale SKHY
quote is carried forward. FX is the latest completed one-minute observation
available at the interval endpoint, subject to a 15-minute maximum age.

Model B begins only after liquidity qualifies for two consecutive completed
intervals:

\[
t_d^*=\min\{t\ge09{:}30:L_{d,t}\ge1
\text{ for two consecutive intervals}\}.
\]

The liquidity score is

\[
L_{d,t}=\min\left(
\frac{DollarVolume_{d,t}}{150{,}000},
\frac{QuoteUpdates_{d,t}}{30},
\frac{75}{SpreadBps_{d,t}}
\right).
\]

Because qualification requires two complete 30-minute intervals, the earliest
possible confirmed liquidity anchor is 10:30 ET. The two reported durations
are

\[
T_d^{clock}=t_d^{convergence}-09{:}30,
\qquad
T_d^{liquid}=t_d^{convergence}-t_d^*.
\]

## Static and dynamic equilibrium references

The static reference applies the causal H1 intercept and slope to the last
available Korean close and current FX observation. H1 parameters for U.S.
date `d` are estimated only from daily rows strictly before `d`.

The dynamic equilibrium incorporates U.S. information arriving after the
open:

\[
\boxed{
a^{*,dynamic}_{d,t}
=a^{*,static}_{d,t}
+\beta_1R^{SOXX}_{d,t}
+\beta_2R^{QQQ}_{d,t}
+\beta_3News_{d,t}
},
\]

\[
\boxed{g^{dynamic}_{d,t}=a_{d,t}-a^{*,dynamic}_{d,t}}.
\]

SOXX and QQQ returns are measured from their 09:30 opens. News is cumulative
from 09:30 through the interval endpoint. Dynamic-reference coefficients are
trained only on prior dates, with minimum requirements of 20 prior sessions
and 200 complete interval observations.

## Thirty-minute error-correction model

For each equilibrium reference and clock, estimate

\[
\boxed{
\Delta g_{d,t+1}
=\alpha+\lambda_{30}g_{d,t}
+\boldsymbol\beta'Z_{d,t}+\varepsilon_{d,t+1}
}.
\]

The control vector contains 30-minute SOXX, QQQ, and USD/KRW returns, news
flow, log SKHY dollar volume, and the bid/ask spread. Only consecutive
30-minute observations within the same U.S. session are used. OLS inference
uses HAC standard errors with two lags.

When \(-1<\lambda_{30}<0\), the simplified model converges monotonically in
expectation:

\[
\frac{E[g_{t+n}]}{g_t}=(1+\lambda_{30})^n.
\]

The implied speed measures are

\[
HL_{50}=30\frac{\ln(0.5)}{\ln(1+\lambda_{30})},
\qquad
T_{90}=30\frac{\ln(0.1)}{\ln(1+\lambda_{30})}.
\]

They are not reported when the estimated coefficient lies outside
`(-1,0)`.

## Direct convergence and threshold model

An interval is inside the equilibrium band when

\[
|g_{d,t}|\le
\max\left(25\text{ bps},Spread_{d,t}+8\text{ bps}\right).
\]

Convergence requires two consecutive completed 30-minute observations inside
the band. Sessions without convergence by 16:00 ET remain right-censored.
Kaplan--Meier output reports median duration and convergence probabilities by
30, 60, and 120 minutes.

The threshold robustness model is

\[
\Delta g_{d,t+1}
=\lambda_{large}g_{d,t}1(|g_{d,t}|>\epsilon_{d,t})
+\lambda_{small}g_{d,t}1(|g_{d,t}|\le\epsilon_{d,t})
+\boldsymbol\beta'Z_{d,t}+\varepsilon_{d,t+1}.
\]

## Current H2 results

The current run contains 1,170 completed 30-minute rows. The static H1
reference is estimable for 47 sessions; the prior-trained dynamic reference is
estimable for 31 sessions.

| Reference | Anchor | Observations | Sessions | `lambda_30` | HAC SE | p-value | Half-life |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Static | 09:30 clock | 517 | 47 | 0.002577 | 0.007746 | 0.7393 | Undefined |
| Static | Liquidity | 517 | 47 | 0.002577 | 0.007746 | 0.7393 | Undefined |
| Dynamic | 09:30 clock | 341 | 31 | -0.019493 | 0.007387 | 0.0083 | 1,056.3 minutes |
| Dynamic | Liquidity | 341 | 31 | -0.019493 | 0.007387 | 0.0083 | 1,056.3 minutes |

For the dynamic reference, T90 is 3,509.0 minutes. Although the adjustment
coefficient is negative and significant, the implied correction is slow
relative to a 390-minute U.S. regular session. Direct event-time evidence is
consistent with this interpretation: 3 of 31 dynamic-reference sessions
(9.7%) enter the equilibrium band before 16:00, while 90.3% are
right-censored. Kaplan--Meier median convergence time is not reached.

The dynamic threshold model estimates an outside-band coefficient of
`-0.020388` (`p = 0.0061`). The small-error coefficient is not stable or
significant. This supports correction when discrepancies are economically
large, but not rapid completion within the same U.S. session.

### H2 conclusion

After the first liquidity-qualified regular-session observation, deviations
from the dynamic cross-market equilibrium exhibit a measurable 30-minute rate
of correction. The estimated adjustment coefficient is
`lambda_30 = -0.019493` (`p = 0.0083`), implying a half-life of approximately
1,056 minutes and a 90% convergence time of approximately 3,509 minutes.
Conditional on semiconductor-market, FX, news, and liquidity information,
9.7% of eligible sessions enter the equilibrium band before the U.S. regular
market closes. H2 therefore supports statistically detectable but slow error
correction; most convergence is not completed within one U.S. session.

## Interpretation safeguards

- Every interval is complete before its endpoint value is used.
- Quotes and news are bucketed over the same 30-minute clock as OHLCV.
- Missing SKHY quotes are not replaced with stale observations.
- H1 and dynamic-reference coefficients use only prior information.
- Static and dynamic references and fixed and liquid clocks remain separate.
- Right-censored sessions remain in the survival estimates.
