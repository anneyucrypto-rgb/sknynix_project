# H3: Does the next Korean session complete the convergence?

## Research question

H3 tests whether a discrepancy remaining at the U.S. close is subsequently
absorbed by SK hynix's Korean ordinary share. This is the U.S.-to-Korea leg of
the asynchronous price-discovery process and does not require Korean intraday
prices or Granger-causality testing.

The canonical implementation is `src/hypotheses/h3.py`.

## U.S.-close equilibrium error

For U.S. session `d`, define the equilibrium error at 16:00 ET as

\[
\boxed{
ECT^{USclose}_d
=\ln P^{SKHY,close}_d
-\hat c_d
-\hat\theta_d
\ln\left(\frac{P^{K,last}_d}{10FX^{USclose}_d}\right)
}.
\]

The sign convention is ADR minus Korean reference. A positive error means
SKHY closes above the estimated FX-adjusted Korean equilibrium. If the next
Korean session absorbs that information, its expected return should be
positive.

The H1 intercept and slope are re-estimated for every U.S. date. Estimation
uses only daily observations available by that U.S. close. The current dated
daily row may be included because it contains the prior U.S. close and the
already-completed Korean session, not the future Korean outcome.

USD/KRW is the latest completed one-minute observation available at the U.S.
close, subject to a 30-minute maximum age. No future FX or Korean observation
enters the equilibrium error.

## Next-Korean-session pairing

The target is the first Korean close strictly after the U.S. information
cutoff:

\[
r^K_{d+1}
=\ln\left(\frac{P^K_{next(d)}}{P^K_{last(d)}}\right).
\]

Korean weekends and holidays are handled with actual observed session dates,
not calendar-day shifts. When several U.S. sessions precede the same next
Korean session, only the final U.S. close is eligible. This prevents the same
Korean return from appearing multiple times as an outcome.

## Regression specification

The estimated model is

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
r^K_{d+1}={}&\alpha+\lambda_K ECT^{USclose}_d+\beta_A r^A_d\\
&+\gamma_S r^{SOXX}_d+\gamma_{FX}\Delta\ln(FX_d)\\
&+\gamma_L\ln(1+DollarVolume^{SKHY}_d)+\varepsilon_{d+1}.
\end{aligned}
}
\]

Here, `rA_d` is SKHY's close-to-close log return. The controls capture the
same-day U.S. semiconductor market, USD/KRW movement, and SKHY trading
liquidity. All are known at the U.S. close. OLS inference uses HAC standard
errors with five lags.

The directional hypothesis is

\[
H_0:\lambda_K\le0,
\qquad
H_3:\lambda_K>0.
\]

The output reports both the conventional two-sided p-value and the
pre-specified one-sided positive-sign p-value.

## Three adjustment periods

| Period | Research question | Canonical analysis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 07:00–09:25 ET | Does SKHY converge during premarket trading? | Not estimated by the revised H2, whose anchor is 09:30 ET; retained as a possible extension |
| 09:30–16:00 ET | Does SKHY adjust during regular U.S. trading? | H2 30-minute ECM and convergence-band analysis |
| Following Korean session | Does the Korean share adjust to information reflected in SKHY? | H3 next-session regression |

This division respects the markets' asynchronous trading hours. H3 isolates
the final leg without treating non-overlapping observations as simultaneous.

## Current H3 results

The current run contains 47 unique U.S.-close/Korean-target pairs from the
causal H1 portion of the sample. The estimated model is

\[
\begin{aligned}
\widehat r^K_{d+1}={}&-0.180627
+0.501834\,ECT^{USclose}_d
-0.149517\,r^A_d\\
&+0.447498\,r^{SOXX}_d
+0.429491\,\Delta\ln(FX_d)\\
&+0.007202\,\ln(1+DollarVolume^{SKHY}_d).
\end{aligned}
\]

| Term | Coefficient | HAC standard error | Two-sided p-value |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Intercept | -0.180627 | 0.292785 | 0.5373 |
| U.S.-close ECT | 0.501834 | 0.159073 | 0.0016 |
| SKHY return | -0.149517 | 0.137063 | 0.2753 |
| SOXX return | 0.447498 | 0.210319 | 0.0334 |
| USD/KRW return | 0.429491 | 0.950576 | 0.6514 |
| Log SKHY dollar volume | 0.007202 | 0.013687 | 0.5987 |

For the directional H3 test, the one-sided p-value for
`lambda_K > 0` is 0.0008. Model R-squared is 0.2615 and adjusted R-squared is
0.1715.

### H3 conclusion

Conditional on SKHY's U.S. return and available semiconductor-market, FX,
and liquidity information, the next Korean session exhibits statistically
significant adjustment toward the cross-market equilibrium. A one-percent
larger positive ADR-minus-Korean log discrepancy at the U.S. close is
associated with an approximately 0.502% higher next-session Korean-share
return (`lambda_K = 0.501834`, one-sided `p = 0.0008`). The current sample
therefore supports H3: a material part of discrepancies not resolved during
U.S. trading is subsequently absorbed by the Korean share. Because the sample
contains only 47 target sessions, this magnitude should be treated as a
current-sample estimate and monitored as more observations accumulate.

## Interpretation safeguards

- Every Korean target close occurs strictly after its U.S. information cutoff.
- Each Korean target session appears at most once in the estimation sample.
- H1 parameters and all controls are known by the U.S. close.
- A positive `lambda_K` has the convergent interpretation only because ECT is
  defined as ADR minus Korean reference.
- Statistical significance does not by itself establish structural causality;
  omitted common information may still affect both markets.
