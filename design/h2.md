# H2: Measure intraday convergence from 07:00 ET

## Purpose

H2 measures how quickly SKHY moves toward the H1 long-run price relationship
after 07:00 America/New_York. It estimates two observation designs and two
equilibrium references so that clock time, market liquidity, FX movement, and
new U.S. information are not conflated.

The canonical implementation is `src/hypotheses/h2.py`. It consumes the H1
daily panel plus point-in-time SKHY quotes, SKHY/SOXX/QQQ five-minute bars,
USD/KRW quotes, the last available Korean close, and timestamped SKHY news.

## Model A: fixed 07:00 observation clock

Five-minute intervals begin at 07:00 ET. For interval `t`, the static pricing
error is:

\[
\boxed{
g^{static}_{d,t}
=\ln P^A_{d,t}
-\hat c_d
-\hat\theta_d
\ln\left(\frac{P^K_{d,last}}{10FX_{d,t}}\right)
}.
\]

$$P^A$$ is the last valid SKHY bid/ask midpoint observed inside that five-minute
interval. A quote must have positive bid and ask and $$ask >= bid$$. If no valid
quote occurs in the interval, the SKHY price and deviation remain missing;
the code never carries a stale SKHY quote into 07:00 or a later interval.

The FX observation is the latest completed one-minute USD/KRW bar available by the end of the interval, subject to a configurable 15-minute maximum age. The Korean price is the latest adjusted close whose 15:30 KST availability time is not later than the five-minute observation. H1 is re-estimated for each U.S. date using daily rows strictly before that date. Thus, $$\hat c_d$$ and $$\hat \theta_d$$  are known before the intraday H2 path.

## Model B: liquidity-qualified anchor

Monitoring still starts at 07:00, but the liquid research clock starts only after the liquidity score reaches one for two consecutive bars:

\[
t_d^*=\min\{t\ge 07{:}00:L_{d,t}\ge1
\text{ for two consecutive bars}\}.
\]

The score is the minimum of three normalized components:

\[
L_{d,t}=\min\left(
\frac{DollarVolume_{d,t}}{DV_{min}},
\frac{QuoteUpdates_{d,t}}{Q_{min}},
\frac{Spread_{max}}{Spread_{d,t}}
\right).
\]

Default thresholds are $25,000 in five-minute dollar volume and five valid quote updates, and a 75-basis-point maximum spread. Because liquidity is confirmed only after two completed five-minute bars, the earliest possible confirmed anchor is 07:10 ET.

The output retains both durations:

\[
T_d^{clock}=t_d^{convergence}-07{:}00,
\qquad
T_d^{liquid}=t_d^{convergence}-t_d^*.
\]

Sessions without a valid liquidity anchor are marked ineligible for Model B; they are not assigned an invented anchor.

## Static and dynamic equilibrium references

### Static Korean-close/FX reference

The static error $$g_{static}$$ is the direct intraday application of the H1 equilibrium using the last Korean close and current point-in-time FX quote. It answers whether SKHY is converging toward the last observed Korean valuation.

### Dynamic U.S.-information reference

The dynamic equilibrium adds information released after the Korean close:

\[
\boxed{
a^{*,dyn}_{d,t}
=a^{*,static}_{d,t}
+\beta_1R^{SOXX}_{d,t}
+\beta_2R^{QQQ}_{d,t}
+\beta_3News_{d,t}
},
\]

\[
\boxed{g^{dynamic}_{d,t}=a_{d,t}-a^{*,dyn}_{d,t}}.
\]

SOXX and QQQ returns are measured from each instrument's 07:00 bar open completed five-minute closes. `News` is cumulative timestamped sentiment from 07:00 through the current interval. The beta coefficients are fitted on prior dates only, with configurable minimums of 20 prior sessions and 200 complete observations. The dynamic reference is a nowcast, not an observed Korean trade.

Comparing $$g_{static}$$ and $$g_{dynamic}$$ distinguishes mechanical convergence from price changes justified by new U.S. semiconductor, market, or news information.

## Five-minute convergence speed

For each reference, anchor, and session, estimate:

\[
\boxed{
\Delta g_{d,t+1}
=\alpha+\lambda_5g_{d,t}+\boldsymbol\beta'Z_{d,t}
+\varepsilon_{d,t+1}
}.
\]

The default point-in-time controls are five-minute SOXX, QQQ, and FX returns, news flow, log dollar volume, and the bid/ask spread. Only consecutive five-minute observations within the same date and session are used. OLS coefficients use HAC standard errors.

When $$-1 < \lambda_5 < 0$$, the simplified constant-equilibrium model converges monotonically in expectation. The remaining fraction after `n` bars is:

\[
\frac{E[g_{t+n}]}{g_t}=(1+\lambda_5)^n.
\]

The reported speed measures are:

\[
HL_{50}=5\frac{\ln(0.5)}{\ln(1+\lambda_5)},
\qquad
T_{90}=5\frac{\ln(0.1)}{\ln(1+\lambda_5)}.
\]

These closed-form measures are not reported when $$\lambda_5$$ lies outside the monotonic stable region. More complex lag structures should instead use simulated impulse responses.

## Direct time to convergence

The time-to-event definition uses an economically meaningful band:
\[
|g_{d,t}|\le\epsilon_{d,t},
\]
where the default band is the larger of 25 basis points or the current spread plus eight basis points of transaction costs. Convergence requires two consecutive five-minute observations inside the band. A one-bar quote flicker therefore cannot create a convergence event.

For each eligible session, the output records the event time, both durations, and whether the observation is right-censored. Sessions that do not converge by the cutoff remain in the sample as censored observations.

`kaplan_meier()` estimates the survival curve and `convergence_summary()` reports:
- median convergence time;
- probability of convergence by 15, 30, and 60 minutes; and
- the no-convergence rate.

Premarket and regular-session rows are estimated separately because the 09:30 transition changes access and liquidity. For regular-session summaries, clock time begins at 09:30; the liquid duration can still preserve the earlier liquidity anchor.

## Threshold ECM
The robustness model estimates separate adjustment coefficients inside and outside the convergence band:
\[
\Delta g_{d,t+1}=
\lambda_{large}g_{d,t}1(|g_{d,t}|>\epsilon_{d,t})
+\lambda_{small}g_{d,t}1(|g_{d,t}|\le\epsilon_{d,t})
+\boldsymbol\beta'Z_{d,t}+\varepsilon_{d,t+1}.
\]
The output flags whether adjustment is faster outside the band and whether each regime lies in the stable interval `(-1,0)`.

## Current H2 hypothesis-test results

The default run contains 9,720 five-minute rows from 2026-07-13 through
2026-11-16. The causal H1 reference is available for 47 sessions
(2026-09-08 through 2026-11-11); the dynamic reference has 45 eligible
sessions after its additional prior-training requirement. These are short
samples, so the findings below should be treated as current-sample evidence,
not as a universal market parameter.

The primary premarket estimates do **not** reject the null of no error
correction:

| Reference | Anchor | Observations | Sessions | `lambda_5` | HAC SE | p-value | Conclusion |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Static | 07:00 clock | 1,314 | 47 | 0.002172 | 0.002189 | 0.321 | Not in `(-1,0)`; no monotonic convergence |
| Static | Liquidity | 1,314 | 47 | 0.002172 | 0.002189 | 0.321 | Same conclusion |
| Dynamic | 07:00 clock | 1,257 | 45 | 0.002083 | 0.006452 | 0.747 | Not in `(-1,0)`; no monotonic convergence |
| Dynamic | Liquidity | 1,257 | 45 | 0.002083 | 0.006452 | 0.747 | Same conclusion |

Consequently, neither half-life nor T90 is reported. The fixed and liquid
ECMs are identical in this sample because every eligible estimation path has
already passed the liquidity anchor before its first usable gap observation;
the two clocks remain distinct in the event-duration outputs.

The direct convergence test reinforces the ECM result. Within the premarket
window, 4 of 47 static-reference sessions converge, giving a right-censored
no-convergence rate of 91.5%. For the dynamic reference, 3 of 45 converge and
the no-convergence rate is 93.3%. Kaplan--Meier median convergence time is not
reached. The estimated probability of static convergence by 60 minutes is
2.1%; for the dynamic reference it is 0.0%.

The threshold ECM also finds no evidence of faster correction for large
pricing errors. The premarket outside-band coefficients are positive for both
references (`0.002299` static and `0.002048` dynamic) and are not significant.
Thus the current data do not support H2's proposed five-minute convergence
mechanism. This conclusion does not contradict the daily H1 cointegrating
relationship: a long-run daily equilibrium can hold even when adjustment is
not detectable during this particular intraday window and short sample.

### H2 conclusion

After the first liquidity-qualified regular-session observation at or after
09:40 ET, deviations from the estimated dynamic cross-market equilibrium do
not exhibit a statistically measurable rate of correction. The estimated
five-minute adjustment coefficient is positive and insignificant
(`lambda_5 = 0.001067`, `p = 0.577`), so an equilibrium half-life cannot be
defined from this model. Conditional on available semiconductor-market, FX,
news, and liquidity information, 9 of 44 eligible sessions (20.5%) enter the
equilibrium band before the 16:00 ET market close; the remaining 79.5% are
right-censored without observed convergence. The current sample therefore
does not support H2's hypothesized intraday error-correction mechanism.

## Interpretation safeguards

- No missing 07:00 quote is replaced with a stale SKHY price.
- H1 and dynamic-reference coefficients use only prior dates.
- The Korean close and FX values are joined by their actual availability time.
- A dynamic equilibrium is an estimated nowcast, not a Korean transaction.
- Right-censored non-convergence sessions remain in survival estimates.
- Static and dynamic references, fixed and liquid clocks, and premarket and
  regular sessions are reported separately rather than pooled silently.
